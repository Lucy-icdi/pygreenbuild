# pygreenbuild

**pygreenbuild** is a Python toolkit developed by the [International Climate Development Institute (ICDI)](https://icdi.network/), a non-profit organization working on climate action, sustainable development, and building-related climate solutions.

## About ICDI

[ICDI](https://icdi.network/) (International Climate Development Institute) is a non-profit organization dedicated to climate development, research, and practice. Through projects such as Green BIM and related climate initiatives, ICDI connects weather and climate information with the built environment to support energy efficiency and carbon reduction.

Learn more about our organization: [https://icdi.network/](https://icdi.network/)

## Green BIM Weather Platform

ICDI’s [Green BIM Building Microclimate Information Platform](https://www.weatherservice.org.tw/) provides weather and climate data for building applications—supporting site-based climate inquiry, TMY3 and related datasets, and tools that help practitioners bring meteorological insight into design and operations.

Explore the platform: [https://www.weatherservice.org.tw/](https://www.weatherservice.org.tw/)

## This Package

**pygreenbuild** is one of ICDI’s ongoing workstreams under that mission. It supports ingesting, processing, and applying weather and building-related data so that climate information can be used in real workflows for building energy and performance analysis.

## Our Goal

Use weather and climate information to help buildings save energy and cut carbon emissions—advancing toward **2050 net-zero** standards.
